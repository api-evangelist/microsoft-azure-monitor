Azure Monitor Monitoring Coverage adds data flow status validation and at-scale recommended alert enablement for virtual machines and AKS resources, enabling teams to verify monitoring configuration effectiveness. The preview feature provides centralized confirmation that signals are collected, alerts activated, and data flows when needed.
